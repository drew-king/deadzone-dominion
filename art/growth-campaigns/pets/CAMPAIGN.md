# Pet collector and powers

- **Audience** · Players drawn to themed collection, hatching and tycoon progression.
- **Promise** · Work toward the first power-themed pet and equip it.
- **A** · [Hatch Your First Pet](pets-a-1920x1080.png). **B** · [Meet Your Power Pet](pets-b-1920x1080.png).
- **Controlled change** · Hatch-action framing versus companion framing.
- **Optional common copy** · Unlock the incubator and hatch power-themed pets. Land on the [public experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/121220316198110).
- **Payoff to verify** · A fresh player finds the incubator, understands the hatch requirement and sees an attainable path to hatching and equipping a pet.
- **Primary** · Plays per impression where attributable, accompanied by CTR and plays per click.
- **Guardrails** · Campaign cohort incubator purchase and first hatch, alongside first production purchase and D1 where measurable.
- **Decision** · Apply the shared test gates. If clicks rise and the cohort consistently misses the hatch path, fix that path before expanding the campaign.
- **Art** · Original hero pet illustration inspired by the power themes. The depicted dragon is illustrative and copy makes no claim about a guaranteed species, rarity or free reward.
