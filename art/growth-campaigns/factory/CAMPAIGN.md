# Factory builder

- **Audience** · Players who enjoy automation and recognizable upgrades. Lead campaign selected by the owner.
- **Promise** · Build a working Scrap factory and expand it floor by floor.
- **A** · [Build a Scrap Factory](factory-a-1920x1080.png). **B** · [Grow Your Scrap Factory](factory-b-1920x1080.png).
- **Controlled change** · Action framing of the headline, with artwork, placement, destination and audience fixed.
- **Optional common copy** · Build droppers, move Scrap, and unlock your next floor. Land on the [public experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/121220316198110).
- **Payoff to verify** · A fresh player independently claims, sees cargo move and sell, collects Scrap and identifies the next production purchase.
- **Primary** · Plays per impression when exposed at creative level, with CTR and plays per click diagnosing the change. Use CTR only as a click-stage result when later creative attribution is unavailable.
- **Guardrails** · Campaign cohort claim, collect, first production purchase, and D1 when sample and attribution permit.
- **Decision** · Use the shared baseline and test gates. Preserve early-session payoff. Keep the result inconclusive if counts or attribution cannot separate the pair.
- **Art** · Original stylized factory illustration. Phase 15 local tests exercised cargo through Floor Three. The tower silhouette, exterior spiral and layout illustrate expansion without promising physical belts on every floor.
