# Deadzone Dominion game design

Deadzone Dominion is an eight-plot Roblox powers tycoon. Players claim a themed plot, build a Scrap factory, unlock gear and floors, and choose between improving the factory, fighting in Reactor Crown, and defending against zombies. Rebirths open additional floors.

## Owner requirements

- Each of the eight power plots has a distinct owner name, crest, accent and recognizable models. Preserve readable routes and daylight.
- Purchases appear after their prerequisites and place only the paid construction. Separate production, weapons, defense, cosmetics, and pets. Keep cargo movement visible through the starter conveyor, extensions, the independent Floor Two line and the Floor Three handoff.
- Use uniform Scrap prices across plots. Target 20 seconds of savings on Floors One and Two, then 32, 44 and 56 seconds for income nodes on Floors Three, Four and Five before documented rounding and balancing.
- Provide a real selectable Arsenal, distinct Cargo Analyzer, roaming zombies with a safe starter route, and base defense with repairable gates and automated turrets.
- Use a 75-space backpack counting a potion stack or unique pet type as one space. Two pet slots are free. Provide cash, speed, damage and armor potions, transparent egg odds, stronger eggs on Floors Three and Five, hatch animation, Triple Hatch and Auto-Open.
- Feature Auto-Collect. Premium pets emphasize income and cosmetic identity. Extra equip slots start at exactly 1,000 Robux globally and double per tier where approved and supported. The current build uses local previews. Preserve a viable free progression path and competitive balance.
- Offer a repeatable 99 Robux Supporter donation. The first processed purchase unlocks a permanent blue nametag. Repeat purchases have the same cosmetic entitlement. List the product in the game shop and on the Roblox experience Store page after fulfillment is verified.
- Use the owner’s Deadzone Dominion Discord for verified-player feedback. Only moderator-approved summaries enter the canonical GitHub roadmap. Review and merge the bot’s roadmap PRs before updating the Dev Hub or planning a release.
- Refresh the Dev Hub roadmap and questions before drafting and executing each phase. Review major milestones independently. Keep production publication and real checkout behind owner approval and recorded acceptance.
