# Roblox tycoon growth research

Research dated November 15, 2026. Audience chosen by the owner is factory and tycoon players. The evidence below comes from public listings, creator notes, original academic studies and Roblox documentation. Neither competitor installations nor the live Deadzone client were available for timed gameplay observations. Public counters, established creator audiences, updates and placement can correlate with success without identifying a cause.

## Psychology applied to Deadzone

| Mechanism and evidence | Application | Falsifiable check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pictures attracted attention in a print-ad eye-tracking study. That format and task differ from a Roblox Home feed. [Pieters and Wedel](https://doi.org/10.1509/jmkg.68.2.36.27794) | One dominant subject per banner. Use foreground cargo, a factory gate or a hatch. Keep the copy on a high-contrast area. | At 160 × 90, ask viewers to identify the activity after a brief glance. Later compare creative-level click and play rates over comparable exposure. |
| Processing fluency research links easy visual processing with positive aesthetic judgments. It offers a hypothesis for hierarchy, contrast and typography. [Reber et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9280.00008) | Short verbs, strong silhouettes, one accent per concept. Keep the same crop across test variants. | Check whether unfamiliar viewers can read the headline and distinguish the three concepts at narrow size. |
| Game motivation studies associate competence, autonomy and relatedness with enjoyment and future play. [Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11031-006-9051-8) | Make the physical sale pay off visibly, explain independent upgrade branches and connect a public defense threat to a route a new player can choose. | Watch for an unaided second purchase or arena/defense return after first sale. Compare cohort outcomes as data accrues. |
| Endowed progress effects were demonstrated in loyalty programs. Applying the underlying progress cue to Roblox is a hypothesis. [Nunes and Drèze](https://doi.org/10.1086/500480) | Show real cargo sold, progress toward the next upgrade and one reachable future floor goal. Give the player a reason to see the next step. | Track claim → sale → upgrade transitions and first-session elapsed times by acquisition source. |
| Message fit is an experimental requirement. A click can coexist with an abandoned session. Roblox advertising standards require accurate representations. [Advertising Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/13722260778260-Advertising-Standards) | Pair each banner with a verified entry point. Keep paid purchase claims, exclusive contents and unmeasured performance out of copy. Original concept art uses the shipped progression themes. | Compare clicked-to-played conversion and first payoff alongside click rate. Hold the build fixed and verify the ad path in the public client before activation. |

## Measurement plan

- **Organic discovery** uses Home recommendation impressions and qualified play-through rate where surfaced. qPTR measures intentional plays on recommendation impressions. It is distinct from paid click-through rate, and Roblox does not disclose a universal qualifying time. Home thumbnail personalization adapts allocation across two to five active images. Roblox staff guidance on low traffic describes meaningful signal, with approximately 1,000 DAU suggested for good results. Treat it as guidance. [Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery) · [Thumbnail personalization](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) · [Roblox staff guidance](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/live-now-personalize-your-thumbnails-to-attract-more-users/3257233/)
- **Paid acquisition** records Ads Manager impressions, clicks, plays, spend and cost per play as defined in the displayed report. Confirm reporting definitions and placement on setup day. The official guide describes Home and Search delivery, 16:9 creative guidance for Home, even distribution across up to ten creatives and reporting lag of up to 48 hours. It also distinguishes **New Players** targeting, which can include people who last played at least 180 days ago, from **New Users** reporting for first-time visitors. Segment reporting windows before comparison. [Ads Manager](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager)
- **First session** measures successful claim, first distinct player collect after a server-credited sale and first production purchase. Keep the Arsenal, hatch and first zombie encounter as independent events where players can reach them in different orders. Do not commit an event when the client merely opens a prompt. Use server-confirmed milestones with short enums and avoid personal fields. [Funnel events](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/funnel-events) · [Journey events](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/journey-events)
- **Return** compare first-time cohorts and traffic sources over complete windows. Acquisition reports provide Users with Plays and source breakdowns where surfaced. Aggregate campaign cohort engagement describes that cohort only. Creative-by-creative first-sale or D1 comparisons require reliable creative attribution. Record launch version, sample, time zone, denominator and attribution before drawing conclusions. [Acquisition](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition)

Measure impressions → clicks → plays before inferring a listing problem. If the public launch fails, fix playability first. If click and play are healthy but first sale is weak, use a first-session observation and milestone evidence to identify onboarding friction. Select the minimum useful effect and required sample before a paid test using the observed baseline, and treat an underpowered pilot as inconclusive.
