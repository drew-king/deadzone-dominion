# Deadzone Dominion — Phases 9–11 execution prompt

Execute all three phases in order in the existing Roblox Game workspace. Read the master design, Phases 6–8, DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md, ALPHA_PLAYTEST.md and the Dev Hub. Start from the current Rojo workspace and use the Phase 8 local candidate as the regression baseline. Preserve the eight plots, profile compatibility and working combat and production loop. Save a separate local Studio candidate for each phase and record which checks actually ran. Cloud publishing and changes to player access require a separate decision.

## Phase 9 — Independent upgrade trees and hidden pads

- Replace the single global purchase order with explicit prerequisites per upgrade. Separate production, weapons and base infrastructure into independently available trees. Keep dropper tiers sequential. A player can buy an available weapon without buying another dropper, and buying a dropper never requires a weapon purchase. Keep any shared floor access requirement visible and separate from branch prerequisites.
- Show the starter choice for each available tree. Until the prerequisite is installed, the next pad, price label, prompt and associated effects are invisible and noninteractive. Hidden pads must have no collision, raycast or touch presence. After purchase, retire the pad and reveal the next eligible choice immediately. Preserve installed equipment and feedback.
- Map existing upgrade IDs to their new trees without removing earned purchases, duplicating charges or silently granting new tiers. Preserve rebirth count and explicitly define the requirements for Floor Two and rebirth. Ensure the requirements cannot strand a player whose old profile already completed a floor. Validate ownership, prerequisites, proximity and payment on the server. Recompute visibility on claim, profile load, purchase, rebirth, release and admin changes.
- Give each branch its own physical area and readable signage. Show available choices and where to collect Scrap. Explain a locked floor at its entrance without showing the individual locked purchase pads.
- Verify fresh and legacy profiles, both purchase orders, hidden-pad collision and visibility, repeated purchase handling, Floor Two, rebirth and two-client plot isolation. Use Studio presets for targeted checks and record real playtest pacing separately.

## Phase 10 — Models, textures and visual gameplay polish

- Start art in Phase 10 with one finished production bay, then apply that standard across both floors, all eight plots and Reactor Crown. Prioritize what players see and use every minute.
- Create and integrate distinct models for droppers, upgrade pads, conveyor assemblies, cargo tiers, processors, sell exchange, collector, claim terminal, arsenal and weapon variants, stairs, rebirth console and arena landmarks. Add texture or decal detail, lighting, readable symbols and animation where they communicate purpose. Give each dropper tier a distinct silhouette and make the cargo-to-sale sequence visible at normal play distance.
- Use original or approved assets and record their source and IDs. Deliver models and textures in the actual local place. Record any missing asset or upload dependency. Keep reusable variants for the eight plot identities and separate complex visuals from simple collision geometry.
- Add clear purchase, sale, equip and rebirth feedback. Improve the first view from spawn, and use signs and landmarks to guide players toward the factory, arsenal, stairs and arena. Check prompt clearance, stairs and rails, camera visibility, collision and coplanar flashing. Compare before and after screenshots from player height.
- Check desktop and available touch/controller paths and measure performance with multiple plots producing. Record uncoached feedback on the first sale, first purchase, time to Floor Two and whether players choose to return to the arena or rebirth. Carry forward the open Phase 8 persistence, multiplayer, performance and release gates.

## Phase 11 — Pets, eggs and balance

- Build a pet loop that players can discover through normal progress. Place an egg station along the factory route and grant the first egg through a clear milestone such as the first production upgrade. Add repeatable earnable eggs through factory and rebirth milestones. Start with a small original roster across Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic. Give each tier distinct silhouettes, readable rarity labels, and clear stats before equipping.
- Give each pet a primary role: multiply factory income or boost damage in supported modes. Start with one equipped pet per player. Apply the income multiplier server-side at the cargo sale payout and define how it combines with rebirth and existing bonuses. Apply damage boosts through the existing damage calculation. Normalize pet damage in competitive Reactor Crown PvP and test the result with two players. Make the strongest paid boosts useful in factory progression or PvE when that mode exists. Include a competitive earnable path.
- Add a pet inventory with hatch results, equipped state, stat comparison, and clear duplicate handling. Show the active bonus in the HUD and give duplicates a defined use. Use lightweight followers with collision disabled so they stay out of camera and movement paths. Avoid paid item trading in this phase.
- Extend profile loading and saving to cover pet inventory and equipped state. Old profiles should load with an empty inventory. Keep earned pets through rebirth and explain that in the confirmation. Isolate Studio presets from saved data. Validate egg cost, unlock eligibility, ownership, duplicate requests and the selected pet server-side.
- Prototype a premium Robux egg with the strongest pets using simulated purchases in Studio. Provide a review table with proposed price, every outcome, numerical odds, effect sizes and an earnable alternative. Verify the [current Roblox paid random item rules](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items) before enabling real purchases. Show all outcomes and actual odds before purchase, check player eligibility through PolicyService and keep the purchase unavailable when eligibility cannot be verified. Use server-side receipt handling that survives retries without granting an egg twice. Keep live spending disabled until the owner approves the egg, balance and price.
- Verify a fresh hatch-and-equip loop, duplicate handling, switching pets, income math, PvP normalization, rebirth retention, old profiles, isolated save/reconnect and retry-safe simulated purchases. Time the first earned egg and ask whether players understand the bonus and want to try another pet. Record desktop, touch, controller and two-client coverage separately.

## Review and handoff

Use a separate critical agent after each phase. Score engagement (20%), retention potential (15%), cleanliness (25%), build quality (30%) and evidence (10%) from 1 to 10. A score of 7 or below triggers fixes and retesting. If it remains below 7 after retesting, rebuild that work and re-review. Apply remaining actionable tweaks and accept only when each category is above 7 and required checks pass. Update development status and the existing Dev Hub with build IDs, evidence, grades and the future prompt responsible for outstanding findings.
